How to use JJ Keys

🎹 The glowing keys are your teacher

In every lesson, the key you should press glows gold. The small number on it is the finger to use (1 = thumb … 5 = pinky). Green flash = correct, red flash = try again.

📚 Follow the stages in order

Stage 1 starts from zero (where the notes are). Each lesson unlocks the next. Earn up to ⭐⭐⭐ — fewer mistakes, more stars.

🎵 Songs wait for you

In song lessons the music waits until you press the right key, so you can go slow. Tap ▶ Listen first to hear how it should sound.

📖 Reading music

From Stage 3 you learn to read notes on the staff. Later, some songs hide the glowing keys so you read the notes yourself. Tap 💡 Hint if you get stuck.

✍️ Note Studio

Pick a note length (whole, half, quarter, eighth), then press a key — the note is written on the staff. Press several keys together to write a chord. Tap a note on the staff to select it and change it. Save your songs and play them back.

💡 Tips

Use landscape. On a phone, use the ◀ ▶ buttons to move the keyboard. Turn note names (A·B) off when you're ready to play like a pro. Practice 10–15 minutes every day — short and steady beats long and rare.
